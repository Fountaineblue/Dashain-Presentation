01 / THE BIG PICTURE

IT'S MORE
THAN A FESTIVAL.

Dashain is one of Nepal's most important festivals, celebrated over 15 days, usually around September or October.

It is a time for family gatherings, blessings, rituals, celebration, and rest.

But ask many Nepalis what Dashain really means, and the answer is simple:

It's about coming home.

02 / THE STORY BEHIND DASHAIN

GOOD WINS.
HOPE STAYS.

Dashain celebrates the victory of good over evil.

One important Hindu tradition remembers Goddess Durga defeating the demon Mahishasura.

Other traditions also connect Vijayadashami with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana.

A celebration of courage, strength, and renewal.

04 / THE HEART OF THE FESTIVAL

A LITTLE RED.
A LOT OF LOVE.

On Vijaya Dashami, younger family members receive blessings from their elders.

Tika: A mark placed on the forehead, often made with rice, yogurt, and red vermilion.

Jamara: Sacred yellow-green sprouts grown during the festival.

Dakshina: Money sometimes given along with blessings — a childhood favorite!

Children flying changa from rooftops. Bamboo swings called linge ping. New festival clothes. Music coming from transistor radios.

07 / THEN, NOW, EVERYWHERE

TRADITIONS
TRAVEL.

For some, Dashain means returning to their ancestral village. For others, it means recreating traditions thousands of kilometers away.

Today, Nepalis celebrate across the world — with family gatherings, shared meals, and even blessings over video calls.

Home isn't always a location. Sometimes, it's the people.
