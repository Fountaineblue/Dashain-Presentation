01 / THE BIG PICTURE

MORE THAN
A FESTIVAL.

Dashain is one of Nepal's biggest festivals, traditionally celebrated over 15 days.

It is a time for family reunions, worship, blessings, celebrations, and the excitement of going home.

For many Nepalis, it brings back memories of school holidays, new clothes, and visiting relatives.

It's about coming home.

02 / THE STORY OF DASHAIN

GOOD WINS.
HOPE STAYS.

Dashain celebrates the victory of good over evil.

One major Hindu tradition honors Goddess Durga and her victory over the demon Mahishasura.

Other traditions also connect Vijayadashami with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana.

A celebration of courage, strength, and renewal.

04 / THE HEART OF DASHAIN

A LITTLE RED.
A LOT OF LOVE.

On Vijaya Dashami, younger family members receive blessings from elders.

Tika: A mark on the forehead, often made with red vermilion, rice, and yogurt.

Jamara: Sacred yellow-green sprouts grown during Dashain.

Dakshina: Money sometimes gifted alongside blessings — especially exciting for children!

Blessings passed through generations.

07 / TRADITIONS THROUGH TIME

TRADITIONS
TRAVEL.

For some, Dashain means returning to the village or family home where they grew up.

For others, it means recreating familiar traditions thousands of kilometers away.

Today, Nepalis celebrate around the world through family gatherings, meals, and even blessings over video calls.

Home isn't always a place. Sometimes, it's the people.
